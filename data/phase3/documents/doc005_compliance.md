# Maintenance Compliance Guide

Document ID: DOC005
Source: FMCSA Maintenance Compliance Guide
Version: 1.0
Status: APPROVED

## Records
Maintenance and inspection records must be complete, accurate, and accessible according to applicable company and regulatory requirements.

## Defects
Safety-related defects must be addressed before equipment is returned to service when required by applicable rules and company policy.

## Documentation
Store supporting maintenance records and required inspection evidence with the appropriate work order or compliance record.
