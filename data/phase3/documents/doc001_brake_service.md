# Brake Service Procedure

Document ID: DOC001
Source: OEM Brake Service Manual
Version: 1.0
Status: APPROVED

## Brake inspection
Inspect brake chambers, hoses, fittings, and related components when a brake fault or driver safety concern is reported. Do not return equipment to service when a safety-critical brake defect remains unresolved.

## Diagnostic sequence
Confirm the reported symptom, inspect the brake system, identify damaged or worn components, perform the approved repair, and complete a post-repair functional inspection.

## Return to service
Record the repair and inspection in the maintenance work order. Equipment may return to service only after the required inspection passes and the maintenance record is complete.
