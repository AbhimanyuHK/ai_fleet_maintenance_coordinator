# Warranty Coverage Policy

Document ID: DOC004
Source: Warranty Coverage Policy
Version: 2.0
Status: APPROVED

## Warranty review
Potential warranty coverage must be reviewed before finalizing responsibility for eligible repairs. Maintain the estimate, invoice, repair notes, and supporting diagnostic evidence.

## Denied coverage
Denied or disputed warranty coverage must be routed to the Fleet Maintenance Warranty or Accident Coordinator with the reason and supporting documentation.

## Evidence
Warranty decisions should not be inferred from a fault code alone. Use the applicable warranty terms and documented repair evidence.
