# Fleet Maintenance Compliance Guide
- Document ID: COMP-FMCSA-001
- Source Type: Compliance Guide
- Authority: HIGH
- Version: 1.0
- Effective Date: 2026-01-01

Equipment must remain compliant with applicable FMCSA requirements and company policies. Maintenance records, inspections, repairs, and supporting documentation must be stored and accessible in the maintenance system. Compliance and safety issues receive priority over routine work.
