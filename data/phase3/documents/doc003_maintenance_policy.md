# Fleet Maintenance Policy

Document ID: DOC003
Source: Fleet Maintenance Policy
Version: 3.0
Status: APPROVED

## Repair approvals
Vendor estimates must be reviewed before repairs begin. Additional work that exceeds the Fleet Maintenance Manager financial threshold requires written approval before authorization.

## Safety escalation
Safety and compliance concerns receive priority. Driver reports must be reviewed and routed according to escalation procedures. Drivers may not authorize financial decisions or repairs for company equipment.

## Work order closure
Work orders must match the final invoice and include applicable maintenance coding, PM reset information, warranty documentation, and supporting records.
