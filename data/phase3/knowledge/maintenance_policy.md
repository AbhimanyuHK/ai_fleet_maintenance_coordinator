# Fleet Maintenance PM Policy
- Document ID: POL-PM-001
- Source Type: Company Policy
- Authority: HIGH
- Version: 1.0
- Effective Date: 2026-01-01

Preventive maintenance should be proactively scheduled at least five days before the due date whenever operationally possible. Coordinate field operations and logistics to minimize interruption. Use approved vendor preference listings when selecting service locations. OEM recommendations and company maintenance policies must be followed.
