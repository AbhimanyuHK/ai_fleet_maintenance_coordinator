# Fleet Warranty Coverage Policy
- Document ID: POL-WARRANTY-001
- Source Type: Company Policy
- Authority: HIGH
- Version: 1.0
- Effective Date: 2026-01-01

Warranty coverage requires review of equipment eligibility, component coverage, mileage or time limits, and repair documentation. Potential or denied warranty coverage must be escalated to the Fleet Maintenance Warranty/Accident Coordinator. Vendor estimates and final invoices must be retained with the work order.
