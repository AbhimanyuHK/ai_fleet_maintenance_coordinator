# OEM Brake Service Procedure
- Document ID: OEM-BRAKE-001
- Source Type: OEM Manual
- Authority: HIGH
- Version: 1.0
- Effective Date: 2026-01-01

Brake fault inspection procedure. Inspect brake chamber condition, air lines, fittings, mounting hardware, and brake response before return to service. A brake performance concern reported by a driver requires safety-priority inspection. Do not return equipment to service when a critical brake defect remains unresolved.
