## Coding principle
Use the applicable VMRS component and repair coding for the completed maintenance activity. Coding should reflect the work actually performed and agree with the supporting work order and invoice documentation.

## Quality check
Before work-order closure, compare maintenance coding with the repair notes, parts, labor, and final invoice.
